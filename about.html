About Me

Hi! I’m Ally.

I’m a multidisciplinary product thinker working across business, technology, and design. I’m interested in how data, systems, and human behavior come together to shape digital products.

My work sits at the intersection of user experience design, product strategy, and technical problem-solving. I enjoy turning complex systems into clear, usable, and meaningful experiences.

With a background from USC’s Iovine and Young Academy, I’ve developed experience in data-informed decision-making, human-centered design, and product development across both technical and business contexts.

Outside of work, I enjoy movies, music, dancing, and creative exploration through gaming and media.
